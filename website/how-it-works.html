How it works

How WinFynd finds the file you mean.

It reads your documents, photos and recordings on this PC, then matches your search by words and by meaning. Every result says why it matched.

    Step 1: Watch

    Windows says what changed

    Windows reports every file added, changed, moved or deleted on your drives, and the index catches up within a couple of seconds. Unchanged files are never read twice.

    It starts with every drive, so the index holds text from anywhere on this PC and keeps it here. Switch off Allow full OS context to pick folders. A check every 20 minutes catches anything missed.

    Step 2: Read

    Text, pixels and speech

    Text page by page, slide by slide and sheet by sheet, in pieces of about 1,000 characters. Photos as pixels. Speech through Whisper, with the time it was said. Up to 48 frames a video through FFmpeg.

    EmbeddingGemma 2 turns each piece of text and each photo into a point in one shared space of meaning, so a receipt PDF and a photo of a receipt land side by side.

    It runs in Ollama on this PC. With the Smallest size only text is placed, and photos are found by name.

    Step 4: Search

    Words and meaning, together

    Every search is matched by its exact words and by its meaning at once. Up to six ranked lists merge into one, so files that do well on both come first, each with the reason it matched.

    Type “pdf from last week” and the type and date become filters you can remove.

    • .wmv

  • Exact words only

    Logs, data and config files are searched by the words in them, not by meaning.

    • .log
    • .json
    • .csv
    • .tsv
    • .xml
    • .yaml
    • .yml
    • .toml
    • .ini
    • .sql

    Found by name only

    • Old Word and PowerPoint files, .doc and .ppt
    • HEIC, TIFF and RAW photos
    • Every other file it watches, by name, folder, kind and year
    • OneDrive files that are only in the cloud, until you download them. Nothing is downloaded to read them.

    Not yet

    No text inside screenshots or scans yet. Photos are matched by what they look like, not by the words in them, and a scanned PDF with no text layer is found by its name.

    Always skipped: Windows, Program Files, AppData, node_modules, build output and caches, and folders that start with a dot (like .git).

Words and meaning

Two ways to match. It tells you which.

Every search runs both at once. Words find the exact terms, like an order number or a name. Meaning finds what you described when the file puts it differently, or has no words at all.

  • Words: sofa, navy

    Words

    The exact words, give or take an ending: “invoices” finds “invoice”, accents don’t matter, and part of a file name is enough.

  • Meaning: a receipt

    Meaning

    EmbeddingGemma 2 compares what you typed with what each page and photo is about, so “receipt” also finds a photo of one.

Clear matches come first. Up to 8 more sit below them, under Weaker matches.

Video frames and less common audio

Takes up to 48 frames from each video so WinFynd can find what was shown, and reads audio formats like WMA and Opus. WinFynd finds it on its own.

No Ollama? It still finds your files.

Ollama is free, from ollama.com, and WinFynd only talks to it on this PC, at 127.0.0.1. Install it later and WinFynd adds meaning to the files it has already read, on its own. Until then the launcher footer says exact words only.

Works without it

  • Exact words in documents, slides, spreadsheets and code
  • File names and folders, even partial ones
  • Speech in recordings and videos, with Whisper

Needs Ollama

  • Search by meaning
  • Photos by what is in them
  • Video frames, which also need FFmpeg
  • Ask

First run

Two minutes of setup. Then walk away.

Setup asks what to search, which search model and which shortcuts. Then WinFynd reads in the background, and you can search while it does.

  3. 3Search modelPick a size of EmbeddingGemma 2, and add Whisper for speech.
