How it works

Describe it the way you'd tell a friend.

Indexed as files appear

WinFynd watches the folders you choose. A PDF you download or a photo you save is searchable a moment later, and unchanged files are never read twice.

Search by meaning

EmbeddingGemma 2 puts text and pictures in one space, so "zebra on a beach" finds the photo as well as the trip notes. Exact words still work too.

Jumps to the moment

Results say why they matched: "Page 3", "Slide 12", "Said at 12:05". The preview opens a video at the frame and a recording at the sentence.

Privacy

Your files stay on your PC.

  • Models run locally through Ollama and whisper.cpp. No account, no cloud index.
  • The index is a SQLite file next to the app settings. Delete it and it's gone.
  • Model files download once when you ask for them. After that WinFynd works offline.

What it reads

Documents, photos, recordings and video.

Documents

PDF · DOCX · PPTX · XLSX · CSV · ODT

Chunked with page and slide labels, so a result points to where the words are.

Photos

JPG · PNG · WEBP · GIF · BMP

Found by what's in the picture. Describe the scene, not the file name.

Recordings

MP3 · M4A · WAV · FLAC · OGG

Transcribed by Whisper on your PC and searched by what was said.

Video and code

MP4 · MKV · MD · HTML · source

Videos by speech and sampled frames. Notes and code by their text.

Setup

The first time WinFynd opens, a short welcome walks you through these steps. You can rerun it any time from the tray icon.

    What WinFynd does, and the promise that nothing leaves your PC.

  2. What to search

    Documents, Desktop, Downloads and Pictures are suggested. Add or untick any folder.

  3. Search by meaning

    Install Ollama if you don't have it, then download EmbeddingGemma 2 with one click.

  4. Shortcuts

    Try Alt Space right there, or record your own combo.

  5. Ready

    Search straight away while your files index in the background.

Questions

Before you install

Does it need the internet?

Only to download models the first time. Searching, indexing and answering all run on your PC, and WinFynd keeps working with the network off.

What is Ollama and why do I need it?

Ollama is a free app that runs open AI models on your computer. WinFynd talks to it on 127.0.0.1 to understand your files. Without it, WinFynd still finds files by exact words and names.

Will it slow my PC down?

The first index takes a while on a big drive. After that WinFynd only reads files that are new or changed. Recordings are transcribed last, because they take the longest.

What about OneDrive?

Files that are downloaded are indexed normally. Files that live only in the cloud are found by name until you open them, so WinFynd never triggers a download.

How much disk space does it use?

The recommended search model is 1.3 GB, with smaller 0.7 GB and 0.3 GB options. Whisper for recordings adds 150 to 575 MB. The index itself is usually a small fraction of your files.
