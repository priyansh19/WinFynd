Videos

Finds the moment in a video

A match can be something said, like 41:18 of the all-hands, or something shown, like the beach at 03:42 of a holiday clip. The preview plays from there.

Searching what is shown in videos needs FFmpeg on your PC.

Search by meaning, photos, video frames and Ask run through Ollama, free from ollama.com. Without it, WinFynd still finds exact words, file names and what was said in recordings.

Ask

Ask a question. Get the answer and its sources.

Press CtrlAltSpace and ask in your own words. WinFynd reads the files that fit and writes a short answer, with numbered sources you can open.

Written on your PC by a chat model you run in Ollama, such as llama3.2 3B. The question and your files stay here.

Live index

New files are searchable in seconds.

WinFynd watches every drive on your PC, or only the folders you pick, and reads each new or changed file within a couple of seconds. Save a PDF or copy photos over and they are in your next search. Recordings follow once Whisper has written down what was said.

  1. 1

    Setup takes about two minutes.

    Choose what to search (the whole PC by default), a search model and your shortcuts.

  2. 2

    The first read runs in the background.

    Search works from the start and fills in as it goes. Recordings and videos come last because they take longest. On the example PC, 51,204 files took 47 minutes.

  3. 3

    Close the window whenever you like.

    Windows tells you when everything is searchable.

Privacy

Your files stay on your PC.

Reading, search, answers and voice all run on this PC. Nothing about your files is sent anywhere.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for, and no analytics.

  • Nothing uploaded

    Your files, your searches and the index stay on this PC. The index holds text from files anywhere on it, unless you switch off Allow full OS context and pick folders.

  • Every connection listed

    Settings shows all three: Ollama on this PC, model downloads when you click Download, and a daily update check you can turn off.

Shortcuts

Four shortcuts, from any app.

They work over whatever app you have open. Change any of them in Settings.

  • Alt+Space

    Search

    Open search over any app and type what you remember.

  • Alt+Shift+Space

    Talk

    Hold the keys, say what you’re looking for, then let go.

  • Ctrl+Alt+Space

    Ask

    Ask a question and get an answer from your own files.

  • Win+Shift+F

    Search the selection

    Select text in any app and find the files about it.
