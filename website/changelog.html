    The first public version. WinFynd reads what is inside the documents, photos, recordings and videos on your PC, keeps up as they change, and finds them when you describe them. Reading, search and answers all run on your PC.

    Runs on
    Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit
    Price
    Free, with its source on GitHub
    Account
    None. Nothing is uploaded.

    Search

    By words and by meaning, with a preview of what matched.

    • Press Alt + Space from any app and describe the file: receipt for the blue sofa. WinFynd matches the words and the meaning at the same time.
    • The preview shows the page, slide or sheet that matched, with the passage, and plays recordings and videos from the matched second.
    • Every result says why it matched, so you can tell exact words from meaning:Exact words on page 3Matches the meaning on slide 7Talked about at 12:05
    • Dates, folders and file types in what you type become filters you can remove. Try pdf from last week in Downloads.
    • Enter opens the file in its usual app. Ctrl + Shift + E shows it in its folder and Ctrl + Shift + C copies its path.
    • Select text in any app and press Win + Shift + F to find the files about it.
    • Reads PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), Excel and CSV, OpenDocument text and sheets, Markdown, HTML, plain text and 35 code and config formats. Every other file is found by its name.

    Photos

    Found by what is in them.

    • Describe a photo and WinFynd finds it by what it shows: dog on the beach at sunset.Image matches your search
    • The preview shows the photo itself, with its size, date and folder.
    • Reads JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP, up to 40 MB each.
    • Needs Ollama with the Most accurate or Balanced search model. With Smallest, photos are found by name.

    Recordings and video

    What was said, and in videos, what was shown.

    • Whisper runs inside WinFynd and writes down what is said in recordings and videos, on your PC. Search when did we agree the Q3 budget and the preview plays from the second it came up.Talked about at 12:05
    • With FFmpeg installed and a search model that reads photos, WinFynd also looks at up to 48 frames of each video, so describing a scene finds the moment it was on screen.Shown at 1:23
    • MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and OGG, and the sound in MP4 and MKV, work as they are. FFmpeg, if installed, covers WMA, Opus, AVI, WMV and the rest.
    • The language is detected for you. The first 4 hours of each file are written down.
    • Turn it on by downloading a speech model in setup or Settings, such as Whisper Small (466 MB).

    Ask

    An answer from your files, with its sources.

    • Press Ctrl + Alt + Space, or start a search with a question mark, and ask in your own words: When does my lease renew?
    • WinFynd finds the six passages that fit best and answers from them, citing each by number with its file and page, slide or time.
    • The answer is written on your PC by a chat model you run in Ollama, such as llama3.2 3B (2.0 GB). Pick one in Settings, or WinFynd uses a chat model you already have.

    Voice

    Say what you are looking for.

    • Hold Alt + Shift + Space, say what you remember, and let go. WinFynd searches for what you said.
    • Whisper turns your words into text on your PC, with the same model that reads your recordings.
    • A level meter shows the microphone is hearing you.
    • In Settings, choose Hold to talk or Press to start and stop, and turn Search when I stop talking on or off.

    Live index

    Picks up new and changed files on its own.

    • Covers every drive on your PC by default. Switch off Allow full OS context in setup or Settings to pick folders instead, starting with Documents, Pictures, Desktop and Downloads.
    • New, changed, moved and deleted files are picked up within a couple of seconds.
    • A full rescan every 20 minutes catches anything missed. Rescan in Settings runs one now.
    • OneDrive files that live only in the cloud are found by name, and read in full once they are on your PC. WinFynd never downloads them to read them.
    • Always skipped: Windows, Program Files, AppData, the Recycle Bin, folders that start with a dot (like .git), node_modules, build output and caches.

    First-run setup and warm-up

    About two minutes, then it reads in the background.

    • Setup asks three things: what to search, which search model and which shortcuts. It takes about two minutes.
    • Pick a search model to run in Ollama: Most accurate (1.3 GB), Balanced (0.7 GB) or Smallest (0.3 GB, text only). Or skip it for now and search exact words and file names.
    • Warm-up then reads your files in the background. Search works straight away and fills in as it goes. Recordings and videos come last, because they take longest.
    • Close the window whenever you like. Windows tells you when everything is searchable: WinFynd is ready.

    Settings

    What to search, models and shortcuts in one window.

    • Choose between the whole PC and the folders you pick, and start a rescan.
    • Pick the search model and its vector size, the Whisper speech model and the Ask model, and see whether FFmpeg was found. Changing the search model rebuilds the index.
    • Record a new combination for any of the four shortcuts. WinFynd tells you if another app already uses it.
    • A Privacy page lists what the app connects to, and About shows your version.
    • Follows Windows light or dark mode and your accent colour, with Mica on Windows 11. Closing the window keeps WinFynd reading in the tray.

    Installer

    For your account, with no admin prompt.

    • Installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinFynd for your account only, so Windows does not ask for an administrator.
    • Start WinFynd when I sign in is on by default, so Alt + Space always works.
    • If your PC does not have Microsoft Edge WebView2, setup installs it first, which needs an internet connection.
    • To update, run the newer setup. Your index, folders, shortcuts and models are kept. Once a day WinFynd checks GitHub for a newer version, and you can turn that check off.
    • Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps. Your own files are never touched, and the index is only deleted if you tick the box.

    Known limits in 0.1.0

    Worth knowing before you install.

    • No text inside screenshots or scans

      Photos are found by what they show, not by the words in them. A scanned PDF with no text layer is found by its name.

    • Old .doc and .ppt files by name only

      WinFynd reads .docx and .pptx. Save older files in the newer format to search what is inside. HEIC, TIFF and RAW photos are also found by name.

    • Not code-signed

      Windows SmartScreen warns the first time you run the installer. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    • Windows only

      64-bit Windows 10 and 11. There is no Mac, Linux or phone app, and no build for Arm-based PCs yet.
