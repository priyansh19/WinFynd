The first public version of WinFynd. It reads what is inside the documents, photos, recordings and videos on your PC, keeps up as they change, and finds them when you describe them. Reading, search and answers all run on your PC, and nothing is uploaded.

**Install:** download `WinFynd-Setup-0.1.0.exe` below and run it. It installs to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinFynd` for your account, with no admin prompt. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

## What's in it

- **Search** by words and by meaning: press Alt + Space from any app and describe the file. The preview shows the page, slide or sheet that matched, and every result says why it matched. Dates, folders and file types in what you type become filters.
- **Photos** found by what they show, like "dog on the beach at sunset" (needs Ollama with the Most accurate or Balanced search model).
- **Recordings and video:** Whisper writes down what is said, on your PC, and the preview plays from the second it came up. With FFmpeg installed, videos are also found by what was on screen.
- **Ask:** press Ctrl + Alt + Space, or start with a question mark, for an answer from your files with numbered sources, written by a chat model you run in Ollama.
- **Voice:** hold Alt + Shift + Space, say what you remember, and let go.
- **Live index:** covers every drive by default (switch off "Allow full OS context" to pick folders instead) and picks up new, changed, moved and deleted files within seconds. Windows, Program Files, AppData and build or cache folders are always skipped.
- **Setup** in about two minutes: what to search, which search model and which shortcuts. Warm-up then reads in the background, and Windows tells you when everything is searchable.
- **Settings** for what to search, models and shortcuts, a Privacy page listing what the app connects to, and light and dark mode with your accent colour.
- **Updates:** run a newer setup over this one and your index, folders, shortcuts and models are kept. WinFynd checks GitHub for a newer version once a day, and you can turn that off.
- **Uninstall** from Windows Settings > Apps. Your own files are never touched, and the index is only deleted if you tick the box.

## Known limits

- Text inside screenshots and scans isn't read yet. A scanned PDF with no text layer is found by its name.
- Old .doc and .ppt files, and HEIC, TIFF and RAW photos, are found by name only.
- Windows only: 64-bit Windows 10 and 11, no Arm build yet.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` holds the installer's checksum. More at https://winfynd.vercel.app.
